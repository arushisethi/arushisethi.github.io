§ 03  ·  Point of view

My interest in AI is narrower than “AI strategy.”

An AI system can increasingly act rather than simply advise.

When it can read an inbox, prioritise information, make a decision and take an action, the question is no longer only what it can do, but who owns the decision. That is the gap between capability and control, and it is the part of this I keep coming back to.

An agent with access to an inbox, a budget, or a customer is useful precisely because it can act without waiting for us. That same autonomy is what makes it harder to supervise. My training makes me ask the less glamorous question: when something goes wrong, who is answerable — and was that decided in advance, or only after the fact?
